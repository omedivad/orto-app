Orto

A cozy logic puzzle in the vegetable garden.

HomePrivacySupportItaliano

Plant garlic, beans, carrots and onions in a garden split into coloured beds. Every row, column and bed needs its plants, and every plant has its needs: garlic loves its space, every carrot wants an onion nearby. No timers, no lives, no rush: one new garden every day and a story of 350 levels.

Orto is free, works offline and collects no personal data.

Support

Questions, ideas or a puzzle that looks wrong? Write to us here (a free GitHub account is needed). Please include your iPhone model and, for a puzzle, the chapter and level number or the date of the daily garden.

Your progress is stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it; "Reset progress" in Settings starts over.
