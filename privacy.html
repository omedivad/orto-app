Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

In short: Orto collects no personal data.

  • No account, no sign-in. You never give us your name, email or any other personal information.
  • Your progress stays on your iPhone. Solved puzzles, seeds, garden and settings are saved only on your device. We never receive them.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising. The app does not use analytics tools, does not track you across apps or websites and shows no ads.
  • No internet connection needed. The puzzles are inside the app or created on your iPhone.
  • Reminders are local. If you turn on the daily reminder, iOS schedules it on your iPhone; nothing is sent to us.
  • Sharing is up to you. When you share a result, the text goes only where you choose to send it.

Children

Orto is a puzzle game suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example optional purchases or ads), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
